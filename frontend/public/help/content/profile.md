# User Profile

The User Profile section in Bowlsman allows you to manage your personal information, preferences, and view your bowling statistics. This guide explains how to use the profile features effectively.

## Accessing Your Profile

To access your profile:

1. Click on your username in the top-right corner of the screen
2. Select "Profile" from the dropdown menu
3. View your profile dashboard

## Profile Overview

Your profile consists of several sections:

- Personal Information
- Bowling Statistics
- Club Memberships
- Competition History
- Preferences
- Account Settings

## Managing Personal Information

### Viewing and Updating Personal Details

To update your personal information:

1. Go to the "Personal Information" section of your profile
2. Click "Edit" or "Update Profile"
3. Modify information such as:
   - Name
   - Contact information
   - Address
   - Date of birth
   - Profile picture
4. Save your changes

### Privacy Settings

To manage your privacy settings:

1. Go to the "Privacy" section
2. Configure who can see different aspects of your profile:
   - Public (anyone on Bowlsman)
   - Club Members Only
   - Team Members Only
   - Private (only you)
3. Save your preferences

## Viewing Bowling Statistics

### Performance Overview

Your profile displays key statistics about your bowling performance:

- Current average
- High game
- High series
- Number of games bowled
- Perfect games

### Detailed Statistics

For more detailed statistics:

1. Go to the "Statistics" section
2. View breakdowns by:
   - Time period (current season, last season, all-time)
   - League or competition
   - Venue
3. Analyze trends in your performance over time

## Managing Club Memberships

### Viewing Your Clubs

To view your club memberships:

1. Go to the "Clubs" section
2. See a list of all clubs you belong to
3. View your role in each club (member, captain, administrator)
4. Check your membership status

### Changing Your Primary Club

If you belong to multiple clubs:

1. Go to the "Clubs" section
2. Find the club you want to set as primary
3. Click "Set as Primary Club"
4. Confirm your selection

## Competition History

### Viewing Past Competitions

To view your competition history:

1. Go to the "Competitions" section
2. See a list of all competitions you've participated in
3. View details including:
   - Date and venue
   - Your scores
   - Your finishing position
   - Prizes won

### Registering for New Competitions

To find and register for new competitions:

1. Go to the "Competitions" section
2. Click "Find Competitions"
3. Browse available competitions
4. Register for those you're interested in

## Account Preferences

### Notification Settings

To manage your notification preferences:

1. Go to the "Preferences" section
2. Select "Notifications"
3. Configure how you receive notifications for:
   - Messages
   - Competition reminders
   - Score updates
   - Announcements
   - System updates
4. Choose delivery methods (in-app, email)
5. Save your preferences

### Display Preferences

To customize your display settings:

1. Go to the "Preferences" section
2. Select "Display"
3. Configure options such as:
   - Default dashboard view
   - Statistics display format
   - Language preference
4. Save your settings

## Account Security

### Changing Your Password

To change your password:

1. Go to the "Account" section
2. Select "Security"
3. Click "Change Password"
4. Enter your current password
5. Enter and confirm your new password
6. Save the changes

## Troubleshooting Profile Issues

If you encounter problems with your profile:

1. **Changes Not Saving**: Ensure all required fields are completed and try again
2. **Statistics Not Updating**: Check that your recent scores have been properly submitted and verified
3. **Profile Picture Not Uploading**: Verify the image meets size and format requirements
4. **Cannot Access Certain Features**: Check your membership status and permissions

For additional help with specific profile issues, please contact support.
