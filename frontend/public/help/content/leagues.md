# Leagues

The Leagues section of Bowlsman helps you manage and participate in bowling leagues. This guide explains how to use the league features effectively.

## Understanding Leagues

A bowling league is an organized group of bowlers who compete regularly over a set period (typically a season). Leagues in Bowlsman have the following characteristics:

- Regular schedule (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly)
- Fixed teams or individual participants
- Consistent scoring system
- Season-long standings and statistics
- Defined start and end dates

## Finding and Joining Leagues

### Browsing Available Leagues

To find leagues you can join:

1. Navigate to the Leagues section from the main menu
2. Use filters to narrow down by:
   - Day of the week
   - Time of day
   - Venue location
3. Click on a league to view its details

### Joining a League

To join an open league:

1. Navigate to the league details page
2. Click the "Join League" button
3. Select whether you're joining as an individual or team
4. Complete the registration form
5. Pay any league fees if applicable
6. Submit your registration
7. Wait for approval from the league administrator

## Creating a League

If you have the appropriate permissions, you can create a new league:

1. Go to the Leagues section
2. Click "Create New League"
3. Fill in the league details:
   - Name and description
   - Schedule (start/end dates, day of week, time)
   - Format (number of games, teams, bowlers per team)
   - Scoring system
   - Fees and prize fund information
4. Set up teams (if pre-determined)
5. Define the schedule of matches
6. Save and publish the league

## Managing a League

As a league administrator, you can:

### Team Management

1. Create, edit, or delete teams
2. Add or remove bowlers from teams
3. Approve substitute bowlers
4. Handle roster changes

### Schedule Management

1. Create the league schedule
2. Assign lanes for each session
3. Reschedule matches if needed
4. Handle position rounds and playoffs

### Score Management

1. Enter or import scores for each session
2. Verify and approve submitted scores
3. Calculate points based on league rules
4. Update standings after each session

### Financial Management

1. Track league fees and payments
2. Manage the prize fund
3. Record expenses
4. Generate financial reports

## Participating in a League

As a league bowler, you can:

### View Your Schedule

1. Go to the Leagues section
2. Select your league
3. Click on "Schedule" to see upcoming matches
4. Note your lane assignments and opponents

### Track Your Performance

1. View your individual statistics
2. Monitor your team's standings
3. Compare your performance to other bowlers
4. Track your improvement over the season

### Submit Scores

If authorized:

1. Enter scores for your team
2. Verify accuracy before submission
3. Review and confirm final scores

## League Statistics and Reports

Bowlsman provides comprehensive statistics for leagues:

### Team Statistics

- Win/loss record
- Total pins
- Team average
- Position in standings
- Points earned

### Individual Statistics

- Average score
- High game and series
- Attendance record

### Reports

- Weekly recap sheets
- Individual bowler reports
- Team matchup history
- Season-to-date standings

## End of Season

At the end of a league season:

1. Final standings are calculated
2. Awards and prizes are determined
3. Season statistics are archived
4. Preparations for the next season begin

## Troubleshooting League Issues

If you encounter problems with leagues:

1. **Cannot Join League**: Check if the league is open for registration and if you meet eligibility requirements
2. **Scores Not Appearing**: Verify that scores were properly submitted and approved
3. **Incorrect Standings**: Contact the league secretary to review the calculations
4. **Schedule Conflicts**: Notify your team captain and league administrator as soon as possible

For additional help with specific league scenarios, please contact your league secretary or administrator.
