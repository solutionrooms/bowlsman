# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

This section provides answers to common questions about using the Bowlsman system.

## Account and Login

### How do I create a new account?

To create a new account:
1. Go to the Bowlsman login page
2. Click "Register" or "Sign Up"
3. Fill in your details including username, email, and password
4. Verify your email address if required
5. Log in with your new credentials

### I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

To reset your password:
1. Go to the login page
2. Click "Forgot Password"
3. Enter the email address associated with your account
4. Check your email for a password reset link
5. Follow the instructions to create a new password

### Can I change my username?

Username changes are typically handled by system administrators. Please contact your club administrator or Bowlsman support to request a username change.

### How do I log out?

To log out:
1. Click on your username in the top-right corner
2. Select "Logout" from the dropdown menu

## Club Membership

### How do I join a club?

To join a club:
1. Go to the Clubs section
2. Search for the club you want to join
3. Click on the club name to view details
4. Click "Request to Join"
5. Wait for approval from a club administrator

### Can I belong to multiple clubs?

Yes, you can belong to multiple clubs in Bowlsman. However, you will have one "primary" club that is used as your default for certain features. You can switch between clubs using the club selector in the navigation bar.

### How do I leave a club?

To leave a club:
1. Go to your profile
2. Select the "Clubs" section
3. Find the club you want to leave
4. Click "Leave Club"
5. Confirm your decision

## Competitions and Scoring

### How do I register for a competition?

To register for a competition:
1. Go to the Competitions section
2. Find the competition you want to join
3. Click on the competition name
4. Click "Register" or "Enter"
5. Complete the registration form
6. Pay any entry fees if applicable
7. Submit your registration

### Why are my scores not showing up in my statistics?

Scores may not appear in your statistics for several reasons:
1. The scores have not yet been verified by a league secretary or competition organizer
2. You haven't bowled enough games to establish an average
3. The scores were entered in a practice session that doesn't count toward official statistics
4. There may be a system delay in updating statistics

### How do I correct a scoring error?

To correct a scoring error:
1. Contact the league secretary or competition organizer
2. Provide details about the error (date, game, correct score)
3. The authorized person can make the correction
4. Once corrected, your statistics will be updated automatically

## Leagues

### How do I create a new league?

To create a new league (requires appropriate permissions):
1. Go to the Leagues section
2. Click "Create New League"
3. Fill in the league details
4. Set up teams and schedules
5. Configure scoring rules
6. Save and publish the league

### How do I join an existing league?

To join an existing league:
1. Go to the Leagues section
2. Find the league you want to join
3. Click on the league name
4. Click "Join League"
5. Select whether you're joining as an individual or team
6. Complete the registration process

### How are league standings calculated?

League standings are typically calculated based on:
- Win/loss record
- Total pins
- Points earned according to league rules

The exact calculation depends on the league's scoring system, which may include:
- Point per game won
- Points for total pins
- Bonus points for achieving certain scores
- Team vs. individual scoring

## Technical Issues

### The system is running slowly. What can I do?

If the system is running slowly:
1. Try refreshing the page
2. Clear your browser cache and cookies
3. Try using a different browser
4. Check your internet connection
5. If the problem persists, contact support

### I'm seeing an error message. What should I do?

If you encounter an error message:
1. Note the exact error message text
2. Take a screenshot if possible
3. Note what you were doing when the error occurred
4. Try refreshing the page or logging out and back in
5. Contact support with the details if the error persists

### Can I use Bowlsman on my mobile device?

Yes, Bowlsman is designed to work on mobile devices through your web browser. The interface will adapt to your screen size. For the best experience on mobile:
1. Use a recent version of Chrome, Safari, or Firefox
2. Ensure you have a stable internet connection
3. Keep your browser updated

### How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

To report a bug or suggest a feature:
1. Click on your username in the top-right corner
2. Select "Help"
3. Click "Contact Support"
4. Fill in the form with details about the bug or feature suggestion
5. Submit the form

## Privacy and Security

### Who can see my bowling statistics?

Your bowling statistics visibility depends on your privacy settings:
1. By default, your basic statistics are visible to other club members
2. Detailed statistics may be visible only to you and club administrators
3. You can adjust these settings in your profile privacy settings

### Is my personal information secure?

Bowlsman takes data security seriously:
- Personal information is stored securely
- Passwords are encrypted
- Access to your data is restricted based on permissions
- The system uses secure connections (HTTPS)

For more details, please refer to the Privacy Policy.
