# Club Management

The Club Management section of Bowlsman allows club administrators to manage all aspects of their bowling club. This guide explains how to use the club management features effectively.

## Club Administration Overview

Club administrators have access to a range of tools to manage:

- Club membership
- Competitions and leagues
- Facilities and resources
- Communication with members
- Club settings and preferences

## Accessing Club Management

To access club management features:

1. Log in with an account that has club administrator privileges
2. Click on "Manage Club" in the main navigation menu
3. Select your club if you manage multiple clubs
4. View the club management dashboard

## Managing Club Membership

### Viewing Members

To view your club's membership:

1. Go to the "Members" section
2. See a list of all current members
3. View member details including:
   - Contact information
   - Membership status
   - Join date
   - Bowling statistics
   - Payment status

### Adding New Members

To add a new member to your club:

1. Go to the "Members" section
2. Click "Add Member"
3. Choose one of the following methods:
   - Create a new account for the member
   - Invite an existing Bowlsman user
   - Accept a pending membership request
4. Fill in the required information
5. Set the member's role and permissions
6. Confirm the addition

### Managing Member Roles

To assign or change member roles:

1. Find the member in the members list
2. Click "Edit" or select from the actions menu
3. Change their role to one of the following:
   - Regular Member
   - Team Captain
   - League Secretary
   - Club Administrator
   - Coach
4. Save the changes

### Removing Members

To remove a member from your club:

1. Find the member in the members list
2. Click "Remove" or select from the actions menu
3. Confirm the removal
4. Optionally provide a reason for record-keeping

## Managing Club Competitions and Leagues

### Creating Club Competitions

To create a competition for your club:

1. Go to the "Competitions" section
2. Click "Create Competition"
3. Follow the competition creation process
4. Set the competition as club-only or open to guests
5. Publish the competition

### Managing Club Leagues

To manage leagues within your club:

1. Go to the "Leagues" section
2. View existing leagues or create new ones
3. Manage league settings, schedules, and teams
4. Monitor league progress and standings

## Club Facilities Management

### Lane Reservations

To manage lane reservations:

1. Go to the "Facilities" section
2. View the lane reservation calendar
3. Add, edit, or remove reservations
4. Set recurring reservations for leagues or practice

## Club Communication Tools

### Announcements

To send club-wide announcements:

1. Go to the "Communication" section
2. Select "Create Announcement"
3. Compose your message
4. Choose the audience (all members or specific groups)
5. Set priority and expiration date
6. Send the announcement

### Email Communications

To send emails to club members:

1. Go to the "Communication" section
2. Select "Email Members"
3. Choose recipients (all members or filtered selection)
4. Compose your email
5. Schedule or send immediately

## Club Settings and Configuration

### Club Profile

To update your club's profile:

1. Go to the "Settings" section
2. Select "Club Profile"
3. Update information such as:
   - Club name and description
   - Contact information
   - Location and address
   - Club logo and colors
   - Social media links
4. Save your changes

### Membership Settings

To configure membership settings:

1. Go to the "Settings" section
2. Select "Membership"
3. Configure options such as:
   - Membership approval process
   - Membership levels and fees
   - Automatic renewal settings
   - Required member information
4. Save your changes

## Club Reports

### Generating Reports

To generate club reports:

1. Go to the "Reports" section
2. Select the report type:
   - Membership reports
   - Competition reports
   - Activity reports
3. Set the date range and parameters
4. Generate and view the report
5. Export to PDF or spreadsheet if needed

## Troubleshooting Club Management Issues

If you encounter problems with club management:

1. **Cannot Access Management Features**: Verify your administrator privileges
2. **Member Changes Not Saving**: Check for validation errors in the form
3. **Communication Not Reaching Members**: Verify member contact information

For additional help with specific club management issues, please contact Bowlsman support.
