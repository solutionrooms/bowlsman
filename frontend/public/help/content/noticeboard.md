# Noticeboard

The Noticeboard in Bowlsman serves as a central location for announcements, news, and important information. This guide explains how to use the noticeboard features effectively.

## Noticeboard Overview

The Bowlsman noticeboard provides:

- Club-wide announcements
- Competition and league updates
- Important dates and deadlines
- Community news and achievements
- Rules and policy updates

## Accessing the Noticeboard

To access the noticeboard:

1. Click on "Noticeboard" in the main navigation menu
2. View the list of announcements in chronological order (newest first)
3. Use filters to narrow down announcements by category or date
4. Search for specific announcements using keywords

## Reading Announcements

### Viewing Announcements

To read an announcement:

1. Click on the announcement title or "Read More" link
2. View the full announcement content
3. See attachments or links if included
4. Check the publication date and author

### Announcement Categories

Announcements are typically categorized as:

- **General**: Club-wide information
- **Competitions**: Updates about tournaments and events
- **Leagues**: Information about league schedules and rules
- **Facilities**: Updates about the bowling center
- **Achievements**: Recognition of bowler accomplishments
- **Urgent**: Time-sensitive information requiring immediate attention

## Creating Announcements

If you have the appropriate permissions (club administrators, league secretaries, etc.), you can create announcements:

### Creating a New Announcement

1. Go to the Noticeboard section
2. Click "Create Announcement" or the "+" button
3. Fill in the announcement details:
   - Title
   - Content
   - Category
   - Expiration date (optional)
   - Attachments (optional)
   - Priority level
4. Select the audience (all members, specific teams, etc.)
5. Preview the announcement
6. Publish the announcement

### Editing or Removing Announcements

To edit or remove an announcement you created:

1. Find the announcement on the noticeboard
2. Click the "Edit" or "Delete" option
3. Make necessary changes or confirm deletion
4. Save changes or confirm removal

## Interacting with Announcements

Depending on the system settings, you may be able to:

### Commenting

1. Scroll to the bottom of the announcement
2. Enter your comment in the text field
3. Submit your comment
4. View other members' comments

### Acknowledging

Some announcements may require acknowledgment:

1. Read the announcement
2. Click the "Acknowledge" or "I have read this" button
3. Your acknowledgment will be recorded

### Sharing

To share an announcement:

1. Click the "Share" button on the announcement
2. Choose the sharing method:
   - Copy link
   - Email
   - Direct message to another member

## Notification Settings

You can customize how you receive noticeboard notifications:

1. Go to your profile settings
2. Select "Notification Preferences"
3. Find the Noticeboard section
4. Choose your preferred notification methods:
   - In-app notifications
   - Email notifications
5. Save your settings

## Noticeboard Best Practices

### For Readers

- Check the noticeboard regularly for important updates
- Use filters to focus on relevant announcements
- Acknowledge announcements when requested
- Save or bookmark important announcements for future reference

### For Announcement Creators

- Use clear, descriptive titles
- Keep content concise and well-organized
- Include all necessary details and dates
- Select the appropriate category and priority level
- Set reasonable expiration dates for time-sensitive information
- Only mark announcements as "Urgent" when truly necessary

## Troubleshooting Noticeboard Issues

If you encounter problems with the noticeboard:

1. **Cannot See Announcements**: Check your club membership status and permissions
2. **Missing Notifications**: Verify your notification settings
3. **Cannot Create Announcements**: Confirm you have the necessary permissions
4. **Announcements Disappeared**: Check if they expired or were archived

For additional help with specific noticeboard issues, please contact your club administrator.
