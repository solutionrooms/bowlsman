# Bowlers

The Bowlers section of Bowlsman allows you to manage bowler profiles, track statistics, and connect with other bowlers. This guide explains how to use the bowler-related features effectively.

## Bowler Profiles

### Your Bowler Profile

Your bowler profile contains your personal information, statistics, and bowling history:

1. **Personal Information**:
   - Name and contact details
   - Home club
   - Years of experience

2. **Statistics**:
   - Current average
   - High game and series
   - Achievement badges

3. **History**:
   - Competition results
   - League participation
   - Score trends over time

### Viewing and Updating Your Profile

To view and update your profile:

1. Click on your username in the top-right corner
2. Select "Profile"
3. View your current information
4. Click "Edit Profile" to make changes
5. Update your information
6. Save your changes

## Finding Other Bowlers

To find and connect with other bowlers:

1. Navigate to the Bowlers section from the main menu
2. Use the search function to find bowlers by:
   - Name
   - Club
   - Average range
3. Click on a bowler's name to view their profile

## Bowler Statistics

### Viewing Statistics

To view detailed statistics for any bowler:

1. Navigate to the bowler's profile
2. Select the "Statistics" tab
3. View comprehensive metrics including:
   - Game-by-game scores
   - Average by league/competition
   - Performance trends

### Comparing Bowlers

To compare statistics between bowlers:

1. Go to the Bowlers section
2. Select "Compare Bowlers"
3. Choose two or more bowlers to compare
4. Select the statistics to compare
5. View the side-by-side comparison

## Bowler Rankings

Bowlsman maintains rankings of bowlers based on various criteria:

### Viewing Rankings

1. Go to the Bowlers section
2. Select "Rankings"
3. Choose the ranking category:
   - Highest average
   - Most improved
   - High game
   - High series
4. Filter by club, league, or time period

### Understanding Your Ranking

Your ranking is determined by:

- Your verified scores in official competitions and leagues
- Minimum number of games requirement
- Recency of scores (more recent scores may have higher weight)
- Consistency of performance

## Bowler Teams and Groups

### Joining a Team

To join an existing team:

1. Receive an invitation from a team captain
2. Accept the invitation
3. Complete any required team registration

### Creating a Team

To create a new team:

1. Go to the Teams section
2. Select "Create New Team"
3. Enter team details:
   - Team name
   - Home club
   - Team members (invite by email or username)
   - Team logo (optional)
4. Save the team information

## Bowler Development

### Tracking Improvement

To track your bowling improvement:

1. Go to your profile
2. Select "Performance Trends"
3. View charts showing your progress over time
4. Analyze patterns in your performance

### Setting Goals

To set personal bowling goals:

1. Go to your profile
2. Select "Goals"
3. Create new goals such as:
   - Target average
   - High game to achieve
   - Tournaments to enter
4. Track your progress toward each goal

## Bowler Equipment

### Managing Your Equipment

To track your bowling equipment:

1. Go to your profile
2. Select "Equipment"
3. Add details about your:
   - Bowling balls (weight, brand, coverstock)
   - Shoes
   - Accessories
4. Record which equipment was used for each competition

### Equipment Performance

To analyze how different equipment affects your performance:

1. Go to "Equipment Performance"
2. Select the equipment item
3. View statistics when using that equipment
4. Compare performance across different equipment

## Troubleshooting Bowler Issues

If you encounter problems with bowler features:

1. **Profile Not Updating**: Ensure you've saved changes and refreshed the page
2. **Statistics Missing**: Check that all scores have been properly submitted and verified
3. **Cannot Find Bowler**: Verify the spelling of their name or try searching by club
4. **Ranking Discrepancy**: Confirm you meet the minimum game requirements for ranking

For additional help with specific bowler-related issues, please contact your club administrator or support team.
