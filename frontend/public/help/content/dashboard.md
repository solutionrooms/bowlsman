# Dashboard

The Dashboard is your central hub for accessing all the important information and features of Bowlsman. This page provides a quick overview of your bowling activities, upcoming events, and recent notifications.

## Dashboard Overview

When you log in to Bowlsman, you'll be directed to your personalized dashboard. The dashboard is divided into several sections:

### Recent Activity

This section displays your most recent bowling activities, including:

- Recent games you've played
- Competitions you've participated in
- Score updates and achievements
- League standings updates

### Upcoming Events

Stay informed about your upcoming bowling events:

- Scheduled competitions
- League matches
- Club events
- Practice sessions you've registered for

### Quick Stats

Get a snapshot of your bowling performance:

- Current average score
- High game
- Recent trend (improving or declining)
- Comparison to your previous performance periods

### Notifications

Stay updated with important announcements and alerts:

- Messages from club administrators
- Competition registration deadlines
- League updates
- System announcements

## Customizing Your Dashboard

You can personalize your dashboard to show the information that matters most to you:

1. Look for the "Customize" or gear icon in each section
2. Select which widgets to display
3. Arrange the order of the sections
4. Set your preferred time period for statistics

## Navigation from the Dashboard

The dashboard serves as a jumping-off point to other areas of the system:

- Click on a competition to view its details
- Select a league to see standings and schedules
- Click on a bowler's name to view their profile
- Use the main navigation menu to access major sections

## Dashboard Tips

- **Refresh**: Use the refresh button to get the latest updates
- **Filters**: Apply filters to focus on specific clubs, leagues, or time periods
- **Expand/Collapse**: Use the expand/collapse buttons to show more or less detail in each section
- **Mobile View**: On mobile devices, the dashboard adapts to show the most critical information first

## Troubleshooting

If your dashboard isn't displaying the expected information:

1. Check your club membership status
2. Verify your league registrations
3. Ensure you have the appropriate permissions
4. Try refreshing the page or logging out and back in

For more detailed information about specific features, please refer to the relevant sections of this help documentation.
