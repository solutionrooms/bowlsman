# Getting Started with Bowlsman

Welcome to Bowlsman, your comprehensive bowling club management system. This guide will help you get started with the platform and understand its core features.

## System Overview

Bowlsman is designed to help bowling clubs manage competitions, track scores, organize leagues, and communicate with members. The system offers a range of features including:

- Dashboard for quick access to important information
- Competition management and scoring
- League organization and tracking
- Bowler profiles and statistics
- Messaging system for communication
- Noticeboard for announcements
- Club management tools

## First Steps

### 1. Create an Account

If you're new to Bowlsman, you'll need to create an account:

1. Navigate to the login page
2. Click on "Register" or "Sign Up"
3. Fill in your details including username, email, and password
4. Verify your email address if required
5. Log in with your new credentials

### 2. Join a Club

To access most features, you'll need to be associated with a club:

1. Go to the Clubs section
2. Search for your club
3. Request to join the club
4. Wait for a club administrator to approve your request

### 3. Explore the Dashboard

Once logged in, you'll see your dashboard which provides:

- Quick access to recent competitions
- Your upcoming events
- Recent scores and statistics
- Notifications and announcements

### 4. Update Your Profile

Complete your profile to get the most out of Bowlsman:

1. Click on your username in the top-right corner
2. Select "Profile"
3. Fill in your personal details
4. Add your bowling statistics and preferences
5. Save your changes

## Getting Help

If you need assistance at any time:

1. Click on your username in the top-right corner
2. Select "Help" to access this documentation
3. Browse the relevant section or use the search function
4. Contact support if you can't find the answer you need
