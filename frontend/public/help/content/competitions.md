# Competitions

The Competitions section allows you to manage, participate in, and score bowling competitions. This guide will help you understand how to use all the competition-related features in Bowlsman.

## Types of Competitions

Bowlsman supports various competition formats:

- **Singles**: Individual bowlers compete against each other
- **Teams**: Teams of bowlers compete against each other
- **League**: Regular competitions between fixed teams
- **Tournament**: One-time or annual events with multiple rounds

## Finding Competitions

To find competitions you can join:

1. Navigate to the Competitions section from the main menu
2. Use the search filters to narrow down by:
   - Date range
   - Competition type
   - Venue
   - Entry fee
3. Click on a competition to view its details

## Registering for a Competition

To register for an open competition:

1. Navigate to the competition details page
2. Click the "Register" button
3. Fill in any required information
4. Pay any entry fees if applicable
5. Submit your registration
6. Wait for confirmation (some competitions require approval)

## Creating a Competition

If you have the appropriate permissions, you can create a new competition:

1. Go to the Competitions section
2. Click "Create New Competition"
3. Fill in the competition details:
   - Name and description
   - Date and time
   - Venue
   - Format and rules
   - Entry requirements
   - Fees (if applicable)
4. Set up the scoring system
5. Define the number of games/rounds
6. Save and publish the competition

## Managing Competitions

As a competition organizer, you can:

1. Edit competition details
2. Review and approve registrations
3. Assign lanes and schedules
4. Enter or import scores
5. Calculate results and standings
6. Publish results
7. Close the competition when finished

## Scoring a Competition

To score a competition in progress:

1. Navigate to the competition
2. Select "Scoring" or "Enter Scores"
3. Choose the appropriate round/game
4. Enter scores for each bowler or team
5. Save after each frame or game
6. Review and confirm final scores

## Viewing Results

To view competition results:

1. Navigate to the competition
2. Select the "Results" or "Standings" tab
3. View overall standings or detailed scores
4. Filter by division, round, or other criteria
5. Export results if needed

## Competition History

To view your competition history:

1. Go to your profile
2. Select the "Competition History" tab
3. View all competitions you've participated in
4. See your performance statistics
5. Compare your results over time

## Tips for Competition Success

- Register early for popular competitions
- Review the rules and format before competing
- Check the venue details and arrive early
- Bring all necessary equipment
- Stay updated on schedule changes

For more detailed information about specific competition types or scoring methods, please refer to the relevant sections of this help documentation.
